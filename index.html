Pricera is a SaaS CPQ software solution designed to automate the manual pricing and quoting processes still prevalent in the B2B manufacturing industry, and in particular in the process manufacturing industry.

Process Manufacturing Industries such as chemicals, coatings, paints, metals, wood, cement, plastics often rely on complex quoting methods for both spot and contract pricing. These industries deal with highly customized products sold in large volumes, where pricing is typically calculated on demand rather than using standardized price lists.

Pricing complexity of the process industry

Pricing complexity arises from various factors, including:
• Raw material costs and fluctuating market prices.
• Intricate costing and pricing models that reflect production complexities.
• Siloed organizations requiring frequent alignment.
• Numerous pricing strategies and contracted terms that vary by product segment, market, and customer.
• A large customer base adding layers of complexity, including transportation, customs, and compliance requirements.
When companies can afford it, they hire large sales teams to manage these challenges. However, when resources are limited, sales teams often rely on cost-based pricing and rough estimates, which fail to capture the perceived value of their offerings.

This leads to:
• Time-consuming and error-prone quotations.
• Lost sales and margin erosion.
• Inefficient workflows that drain up to 50% of sales resources.
• Disruption costs that further erode profitability.

How we solve it

Pricera eliminates the need for cumbersome spreadsheets, manual pricing rules, contracts, PDFs, lengthy alignment meetings, and corrective calls. Instead, it provides a single, automated SaaS tool that streamlines pricing and quoting processes for sales teams across plants, divisions, and countries.
Pricera can function as a standalone solution or integrate seamlessly with existing CRM/ERP systems, adapting to the unique sales processes and product complexities of each company.

Benefits

• Faster Quotes: Reduces quotation times by over 90%, shrinking sales cycles from months, weeks, or days to just a few minutes.
• Improved profitability: Pricera boost revenue and operating profits with more quotes generated and optimized pricing models
• Consistent Pricing: Ensures uniform pricing across markets, customers, and sales teams.
• Dynamic and Accurate Pricing: Handles complex value-based pricing models, incorporating factors like raw material costs and foreign exchange rates.
• Policy Compliance: Guarantees adherence to pricing policies, discount rules, and contractual terms (e.g., MOQ, tolerances, validity), ensuring robust and error-free proposals.
• E-Procurement Enablement: Allows customers to self-enquire about pricing and terms, ensuring fully compliant orders.
• Data Capture: Tracks valuable sales data for traceability and continuous improvement.
• Machine Learning: Optimizes pricing models, measures price elasticity, and refines customer and product segmentation.
• Ease of Use: Minimal training required—new sales recruits can quote effectively almost immediately instead of several months for highly technical products.

Our differentiation

Industry Focus: Pricera specifically addresses the pricing complexities of the $7 trillion process manufacturing industry, a market underserved by traditional pricing software vendors.
Lightweight and Efficient: Unlike bulky, one-size-fits-all CPQ solutions, Pricera is natively designed for the intricate pricing models of the process industry. It eliminates the need for heavy configurations and lengthy certification sessions.
Non-Disruptive Implementation: Pricera replicates existing pricing models, preserving their uniqueness and complexity while streamlining workflows. The software adapts to the company’s specific sales processes and products.
Pricing Optimization: Pricera’s optional AI and machine learning features enhance pricing strategies, optimize models, and support continuous improvement once the system is adopted.
